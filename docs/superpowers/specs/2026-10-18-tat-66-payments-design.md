## Goal

The owner opens a project, sees the client's payment screenshot next to the amount the deposit page asked for and the project's reference code, checks the studio's eSewa statement and taps Verify; the project books once a session is scheduled. At the desk the owner records cash, bank or QR payments, refunds and reversals of mistakes, never editing a row. The project and the client's profile show the balance due. When a project closes holding a deposit, the owner records whether the studio kept or refunded it.

Written without the user (unattended run): every open question is a ruling below. **Two migrations** (payment corrections and notes; `deposit_outcomes`).

## Rulings

1. **Scope.** Owners decide proofs, record desk payments, refunds and reversals, and record deposit outcomes on closed projects; the price of a direct booking made without one can be set once; the balance due shows on the project and the client. The project page gets a Payments card, the projects list two owner flags, the client profile a Payments section. Session outcomes (TAT-85), gateway confirmation (TAT-72), alert delivery (TAT-52), the storefront page (TAT-65b), reports (TAT-76) and customer messages are other tickets (see "Not in this ticket").
2. **A correction names what it reverses.** `payments.corrects_payment_id uuid null`, a composite key `(tenant_id, project_id, corrects_payment_id)` to `payments (tenant_id, project_id, id)` (a new unique), `ON DELETE NO ACTION`; `CHECK ((kind = 'correction') = (corrects_payment_id is not null))`, `CHECK (corrects_payment_id <> id)`, and a unique index on `corrects_payment_id` (one reversal per payment). The guard freezes it after insert. **Deposit money** is the verified `deposit` payments plus only the corrections whose corrected payment is a deposit, in the quote's currency (`depositMoney` in `#modules/payments/ledger`); `DepositService.verifiedPaid` uses it. This fixes TAT-36's latent bug, test-first: a NPR 6,000 deposit, NPR 3,000 paid, a mistaken refund and its reversal counted NPR 6,000 and booked the project underfunded.
3. **"Reverse" ships now.** An owner reverses any verified payment that isn't a correction and hasn't been reversed, in full: a `correction` of minus its amount, in its currency and method, verified by that owner at once, with an optional reason. It is the only fix for a mistaken verification or a mistyped desk entry (decided payments are final), and without it `corrects_payment_id` would be dead code. A reversal reversed by mistake is fixed by recording the payment again. Rejected: partial corrections (a reversal plus a new entry says the same); reversing a reversal (chains nobody can read).
4. **A reversal never changes the project's status.** A booked project whose deposit is reversed stays `booked`; the Payments card says the deposit is no longer covered and what is due. A `deposit_pending` project then books only once the deposit is covered again. Rejected: un-booking (the session is real and the client may pay at the desk; an automatic move would cancel nothing and confuse the board).
5. **Expiry and late decisions (TAT-83).** The quote's expiry also spares a project while a deposit screenshot waits for the studio, and once the studio rejects a deposit screenshot or reverses a deposit at or after the current quote's expiry, by the decision's `verified_at` (`decidedLate` in the ledger, `DepositService.expirySpared`, checked first in `ProjectService.expiryCancels`). A rejection or reversal made before the expiry leaves the expiry to act as usual; a revision issues a new quote with its own expiry. Nothing extends or clears an expiry (quotes never change). The outcome no longer depends on when the sweep runs: the owner who decided late chooses what happens next (revise the quote, take the payment at the desk, or withdraw). Rejected: letting the sweep cancel (whether it does depends on how late the owner decided, and it cancels the sessions minutes after a bookkeeping fix); extending the expiry (quotes are immutable).
6. **Verify and reject.** Owners only. Under the project's row lock and then the payment's, the payment must still be `submitted`, else `already_decided` ("someone already decided it"; covers a double tap and two owners). The decision sets `status`, `verified_at`, `verified_by_user_id` and an optional note. Verifying a deposit calls `settleBooking`; the flash says what happens next (booked, schedule a session, or what is still due). If the client paid a different amount, the owner rejects and records what arrived (TAT-37). The proof file stays. Rejected: "verify a different amount" in one step (a second path for a rare case that reject plus record covers).
7. **`payments.note` varchar(500).** Written once: at insert (desk payments, refunds, reversals) or in the update that decides a submitted payment; after that it can only be cleared (TAT-42's privacy deletion), like the proof. `payments_guard` is replaced to allow exactly that, keeping its pinned `search_path`. Only staff see it.
8. **Desk payments.** Kinds: `deposit` only while the project is `deposit_pending` (then `settleBooking`), `session` and `balance` in any status but `declined`. Methods: cash, bank, Fonepay, eSewa, Khalti. Amount NPR 0.01 to 1,00,00,000 with at most two decimals (hourly totals have paisa). Recorded `verified` at once by the recording owner, in the current quote's currency (NPR without a quote). Overpaying is allowed and shows as overpaid. The button disables while saving; a duplicate is reversed. Rejected: an idempotency key (a column and a code path for a rare case that Reverse fixes); refusing overpayment (clients do pay the whole price up front).
9. **Refunds.** Owners record a negative `refund` up to what the project holds (its net verified payments in its currency), with method and note, in any status. On a closed project that still needs its deposit outcome, the outcome form is the way (`outcome_first`), so a refund is never recorded twice. Refunds don't count against the deposit: booking is unaffected and the balance due grows by the refund.
10. **Balance due** = the current quote's total (the fixed price, or the hourly rate × estimated minutes / 60 in bigint, rounded half away from zero: `quoteTotal`) − every verified payment in the quote's currency, signed (refunds and corrections included); submitted and rejected payments and other currencies don't count. Computed on read, never stored, in bigint. States: due, paid, overpaid, no price; a declined or cancelled project shows what it holds instead. Hourly projects use the estimate (known limit, see Carry-forwards). The client's total is the sum of the positive balances of their projects that aren't declined or cancelled.
11. **The price of a direct booking (TAT-43).** On a booked-or-later project with no quote, "Set the price" inserts quote version 1: fixed, deposit 0, valid through today, no `ExpireQuoteJob`, no status change (`ProjectService.setPrice`, `QuoteRuleError` `price_locked` otherwise). Owners and the assigned artist, as for quotes. Once only, behind a confirm, so "quotes lock at booked" stays true. Rejected: a price column on the project (a second source of price); revisions after booking.
12. **Deposit outcomes.** A `deposit_outcomes` row (payments module, tenant-owned with RLS, not soft-deleted): `outcome` (`kept`, `moved`, `refunded`), `amount` (the deposit money held when recorded, > 0) and `currency`, `refund_payment_id` (the refund, same project; set exactly for `refunded`; used once), `session_id` (same project; TAT-85's no-show or cancelled session; `moved` needs one), `note`, `recorded_by_user_id` (`SET NULL`). One per project close (`session_id` null) and one per session (partial uniques). Append-only by convention: the service only inserts (as for quotes); the money itself is a guarded payment. This ticket records it on a `cancelled` or `declined` project holding deposit money: kept, or refunded with a refund payment up to the deposit held (partial allowed: the rest is kept), in one transaction. TAT-85 records no-shows and moves with `session_id`. Rejected: a column on projects (a no-show doesn't close the project); a guard trigger (it guards a label, not money).
13. **Withdrawing with a deposit held (TAT-46).** `move("withdraw")` no longer refuses `deposit_paid` (the reason goes), and `withdraw` is allowed from `booked` and `in_progress` too. The confirm names the deposit, and the flash and the Payments card ask for the outcome. Rejected: the outcome inside the withdraw dialog (expiry and late verifications reach the same state, so the Payments card needs the form anyway; one form, one route).
14. **Who sees and does what.** Owners do everything here: the `/t/:tenant/payments` routes are an owner-only group, and the project routes' service refuses anyone else with the 404 of a project that isn't theirs (TAT-65's precedent). The assigned artist sees a read-only Payments card (balance, deposit line, the list; no screenshots, no actions, no outcome form), so they can tell a client at the session what is left. The client profile stays owners only. `docs/requirements.md` then says payment proofs are owners only. Rejected: hiding payments from artists (they take the balance at the session); showing them screenshots (the client's account details; the artist decides nothing).
15. **Proof images.** `ImageService.url(purpose, key, tenant)` asserts `tenants/<tenant id>/<purpose>/` before signing (every purpose; the callers pass their tenant; a mismatch is a server error), as defence in depth behind the tenant-scoped row load, which stays the access check. Private signed URLs carry `cacheControl: "private, no-store"`, which the drive's `/files` route sends as `Cache-Control` and which the signature covers. Proof URLs are signed by `ProjectsController` for owners only, after `PaymentService.forProject` loaded the payments through the tenant-scoped project, and appear only in that page's props (5 minutes), never in jobs, logs or messages. Rejected: an app route streaming the proof (the signed route already checks).
16. **Where it shows.** The Payments card on the project page (screen below); the projects list flags, for owners, "screenshot to check" (a deposit proof waits) and "deposit to settle" (a closed project holds deposit money with no outcome), so the Projects badge leads somewhere; the client profile's Payments section, from `PaymentService` reads (the clients module reads no payments); TAT-65's Deposit card points to the Payments card. Deciding a proof drops `proofCount`. Rejected: balance on the board (artists share it; a read per row).
17. **Who decided.** Rows say "Verified by <name>" ("Recorded by" for a desk entry, which has no screenshot) with the user's full name, else their email; a verifier whose account was deleted reads "a deleted account". Users have no module service, so `PaymentService.memberNames` reads `User` by id.

## Review amendments

Made after the Codex and Fable reviews of the finished branch. They change wording of rulings above; where they differ, this section wins.

- **Ruling 5 (expiry and late decisions): any decision on the deposit made at or after the expiry spares the project, not only a rejection or a reversal.** `decidedLate` also counts a deposit verified or recorded at the desk at or after the current quote's expiry (by its `verified_at`). Before: a partial deposit the owner recorded an hour after the expiry did not spare the project, and the next sweep run (within 5 minutes) cancelled it holding verified money. Now the owner who took the money chooses what happens next, as for a late rejection or reversal. A deposit taken before the expiry acts as before (a settled deposit is never cancelled; an unsettled one is cancelled by the expiry), session, balance and refund payments are not decisions about the deposit, and a revision's new expiry starts the comparison again. Rejected alternative: leaving the sweep to cancel, which is the outcome ruling 5 set out to avoid.
- **Ruling 16 (where it shows): a third owner flag on the projects list, "quote expired".** A deposit_pending project whose current quote has expired and which the sweep spares because of a late decision on its deposit (ruling 5) has no waiting screenshot and no deposit to settle, so nothing on the list said it waits for the owner. `PaymentService.listFlags` now returns `quoteLapsed` for it (owners only, not while a screenshot waits) and the list shows a `Badge` "quote expired": revise the quote, take the rest at the desk or withdraw.
- **Rulings 9 and 12 (refunds, deposit outcomes): the outcome is about the deposit the project still holds.** `settleableDeposit` = the smaller of the deposit money and what the project holds (net verified payments), never below zero. It is the limit of an outcome's refund (`refund_too_large` carries it), the amount the outcome stores, and the gate of "needs an outcome" (the Payments card, the `outcome_first` refusal and the "deposit to settle" flag). Before: a deposit refunded at the desk before a withdrawal still counted as held, so the outcome form refunded it a second time (net -NPR 3,000) or recorded a false "kept". A deposit refunded in full needs no outcome; a deposit refunded in part is about the rest. Refunds still don't count against the deposit for booking (ruling 9 stands).
- **Ruling 12: `deposit_outcomes.amount` is a bigint.** It stores a sum of deposits, which can pass int4 (each payment is capped at 1e9 paisa, but desk deposits pile up while a project waits for a session); `DepositOutcome` reads it back as a number.
- **Ruling 10 (screens): a closed project that refunded more than it received reads "Refunded NPR x more than it received.", not "Holds -NPR x".**
- **Ruling 14 (who does what): who may act is decided before the body is validated** (`PaymentService.requireOwner` in the three project routes), so the assigned artist gets the 404 whatever they post.
- **Screens: the line under a waiting screenshot reads "the quote or the payments received changed after this was sent"** (an owner recording part of the deposit at the desk is not a quote change), and the Record payment form restarts (a React `key` from the offered kinds and suggested amounts) when those change, e.g. after a deposit books the project.
- **Migration 1791660000000 refuses to apply or roll back over existing corrections** (documented in `database/README.md`); rolling back would drop the payments the reversals name.
