# Staff alerts and one-tap message templates (TAT-52)

Date: 2026-10-19
Refs: TAT-52 (Staff new-inquiry alerts + one-tap WhatsApp/Viber message templates). Builds on TAT-7 (the worker and the Postgres queue, `TenantJob`, `dispatchInTransaction`), TAT-6 (`@adonisjs/mail`, `SendMailJob`, the shared email layout, Mailpit in development), TAT-46 (the inbox, the project page, "Mark contacted" and `tattoo_projects.last_contacted_at`), TAT-64 (`quoteMessage`, `chatLinks`, the quote's chat message), TAT-65 (`depositMessage`, the deposit card, `DepositProofAlertJob`), TAT-51 (`NewInquiryAlertJob`), TAT-67 (the app shell), TAT-41 (the client profile's chat links), TAT-85 (sessions on the project page) and TAT-38 (the deploy checklist's "the owner gets no signal").
Size: **about 5 points** (the ticket says 3). Ruling 1 keeps it there by moving the deposit-proof alert, email preferences and retention to carry-forwards.

## Goal

When a client sends an inquiry, the studio's owners, and the artist the client asked for, hear about it: an email from the worker and an alert in the dashboard. On every project, and on a new inquiry, staff open the client's WhatsApp or Viber in one tap, with a message the studio wrote once: inquiry received, quote, deposit link, booking confirmation, session reminder or aftercare. Each tap records when the client was last contacted, on which channel and with which message.

Three additive migrations (the last-contact columns on `tattoo_projects` and `inquiries`, `message_templates`, `staff_alerts`) and one new optional setting (`ALERT_EMAILS`). No API, OpenAPI, SDK or storefront change. Six new dashboard routes. Written without the user (unattended run): every open question is a ruling below.

## Depends on the built chain

Designed on e2b3c1d, the tip of the whole stacked chain (TAT-24 to TAT-66, TAT-12, 23, 38, 44, 48, 55, 57, 9, 85 and 65b). It holds `NewInquiryAlertJob`, `DepositProofAlertJob`, the deposit card, TAT-85's Sessions card and TAT-9's cookie policy, which this ticket edits next to. The plan's Task 0 stops on any other base.

## Rulings

1. **The smallest slice that meets the four boxes, about 5 points.** In: (1) a new-inquiry email and in-app alert to the owners and the preferred artist, sent from the worker; (2) one-tap WhatsApp and Viber links on every project and on a new inquiry; (3) six editable message templates per studio, with defaults; (4) the last contact recorded when a link is tapped. Out, as carry-forwards: the deposit-proof alert (ruling 12), a per-member email preference (ruling 8), pruning old alerts (ruling 11), an "intake form at its limit" alert (ruling 13), automatic sending of any client message (ruling 26), who made the last contact (ruling 27), templates on the client profile (ruling 15). Why: the ticket is 3 points and every piece above is needed for one of its boxes. Rejected: also building the deposit-proof alert (the in-app part already exists as the Projects badge, and its email can't reach anyone before the sending domain, TAT-20, exists).
2. **One new module, `notifications`, owns alerts and message templates.** `app/modules/notifications/` holds `StaffAlert` (table `staff_alerts`), `MessageTemplate` (table `message_templates`), `AlertService`, `MessageTemplateService`, the pure `messages.ts` (kinds, placeholders, defaults, the renderer), `alerts.ts`, `chat.ts` and `project_messages.ts`, the `NewInquiryMail` email and the validators. The architecture's module table already names it ("Notifications: staff alerts, click-to-chat templates"). The table is `staff_alerts`, not `notifications`: Lucid names a model's table after the class, and a model called `Notification` would read like the browser's global of the same name. Rejected: putting templates in `projects` (the inquiry page uses them too) or alerts in `inquiries` (the follow-up's deposit kind belongs to neither).
3. **Who is alerted to a new inquiry.** Every live owner membership of the studio, plus the member linked to the inquiry's preferred artist when that artist profile is live, linked to a user (`artists.user_id`), and that user has a live membership in the studio (any role). One alert per user: an owner who is also the preferred artist gets one, which says they were asked for. Removed members, unlinked or deleted artist profiles and other studios' members get nothing. An inquiry that is gone or already decided when the job runs alerts nobody (TAT-51's check stays). Rejected: every member (artists who weren't asked can't open the inbox); the artist alone when one was asked for (owners decide inquiries).
4. **Two channels per alert: an email from the worker and an in-app alert.** No SMS, no browser push, no realtime count (the count refreshes on the next page load). Rejected: web push (a service-worker subscription, VAPID keys and a permission prompt for a 3-point ticket); SMS (V1, costs money per message).
5. **What an alert may contain.** The email: the studio's name, the client's first name (as the inquiry gives it), the first 140 characters of the idea (whole graphemes, whitespace collapsed, "…" when cut), which artist was asked for, and, for owners, a link to the inquiry on the dashboard (`APP_URL` + `/t/<slug>/inbox/<id>`, which needs a signed-in owner). Never: the phone, the email address, the budget, the placement, the references, a last name, any deposit link, token, amount or reference code (TAT-65's rule, kept for every future kind). The subject and preheader carry no client data at all ("New inquiry for {studio}"), because `SendMailJob.failed()` logs the subject. The in-app row stores ids only (member, kind, inquiry); its list shows a fixed line per kind and the time, so no client data is copied into it and TAT-42's privacy deletion has nothing to scrub there. Rejected: the client's first name in the in-app list (one read per page of another module's rows, for a list that is one tap from the inbox); the whole idea in the email (an inbox keeps it forever).
6. **Exactly one alert and one email per member per inquiry.** `staff_alerts` has a unique `(user_id, inquiry_id)`; `AlertService.newInquiry` inserts every recipient with `insert … on conflict do nothing returning user_id` and queues an email (`SendMailJob`) only for the rows inserted now, in the same transaction (`sendMailInTransaction`, a new `#services` helper that does what `mail.sendLater` does but through `dispatchInTransaction`). A retried job, a job the worker reran after a crash, or two workers running it at once add no second alert and queue no second email; a failure anywhere rolls back both. The SMTP send itself is at least once (`SendMailJob` retries), as for every email. Rejected: `mail.sendLater` (queues outside the transaction, so a rollback after it would leave an email for an alert that doesn't exist and the retry would send another); a "sent" flag checked before sending (racy).
7. **Mail without a sending domain.** The existing settings stay (`MAIL_MAILER`, `MAIL_FROM_NAME`, `MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`, `SMTP_*`; mail classes never set `from`). New optional `ALERT_EMAILS` (boolean, unset means on): `false` keeps every in-app alert and queues no email, and the web server, the worker and every ace command log one warning at boot ("Staff alert emails are off (ALERT_EMAILS=false): new inquiries alert the dashboard only."). SMTP failures stay inside `SendMailJob`: 3 retries, then one error log line per email; the inquiry was committed long before and the alert job never fails because of mail. Production needs the platform's sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC (TAT-20); until then set `ALERT_EMAILS=false` or point `SMTP_*` at a provider that may send for `MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS`. Alerts go to the account's email as it is (verification is TAT-18). Rejected: failing the alert job on a mail error (it would retry the in-app alert too); no switch (every inquiry would end in failed mail jobs and error logs until TAT-20).
8. **No email preference per member in this ticket.** Every recipient of ruling 3 gets the email; the email says why ("You get these emails as a member of {studio} on Tattoo Drip."). A per-member "email me about new inquiries" switch is a carry-forward, with the place it belongs (the Alerts page, which every member can open). Why: the pilot studio has one or two owners, and `ALERT_EMAILS` covers the platform-wide off switch. Rejected: a preference now (a column, a form, a route and its isolation case for no current user).
9. **In-app alerts: a list and a link with the unread count.** The shell gets an "Alerts" link: a bell icon with the count in the phone's top bar, next to the account menu, and a row with the count in the desktop sidebar, above the account menu (not a tab: the tab bar keeps its destinations). It opens `/t/:tenant/alerts`, the member's own alerts in this studio, newest first, 25 a page; unread ones are marked with a dot and "Unread" for screen readers. The count is a new shared prop, `alertCount` (the member's unread alerts in this studio; null off tenant pages), for owners and artists alike. The lines: owners "New inquiry" / "Open it in the inbox."; a non-owner (the preferred artist) "A new inquiry asks for you" / "An owner reviews it in the inbox."; then the time ("Today, 14:05", "Yesterday, 09:12", "12 Oct, 09:12", "12 Oct 2025, 09:12", in the studio's timezone). Rejected: a dropdown under the bell (the shell would read the alerts on every page); counting new inquiries again (the Inbox badge already does, and an artist has no inbox).
10. **Opening an alert marks it read; "Mark all as read" marks the rest.** Each row is a button that posts to `POST /t/:tenant/alerts/:id/open`: it marks the member's own alert read and redirects to its target, computed on the server from the row (an owner's new-inquiry alert: the inquiry's inbox page; anyone else: back to the list), never from the request, so there is no open redirect. `POST /t/:tenant/alerts/read` marks all the member's unread alerts in this studio read ("All alerts marked as read."). Another member's alert, even in the same studio, is the same 404 as another studio's. Handling an inquiry from the inbox doesn't mark its alert read (the Inbox badge is what counts work left). Rejected: marking alerts read when the list is viewed (a GET that writes); marking them read when the inquiry page is opened (another module writing alerts on every inquiry view).
11. **Alerts are kept.** No pruning in this ticket: a studio with 50 inquiries a day and 3 recipients adds about 55,000 small rows a year, the list is paginated and the count uses a partial index. A prune job (for example read alerts older than 90 days) is a carry-forward. Rejected: deleting on insert (a write the alert job doesn't need).
12. **The deposit-proof alert is not in this ticket (TAT-65's carry-forward, declined).** `DepositProofAlertJob` keeps logging ids only, and the Projects badge (`proofCount`) stays the owners' in-app alert, as TAT-65 shipped it. The follow-up adds the `deposit_proof` kind to `staff_alerts` (nullable `project_id` and `payment_id` with composite keys, a unique `(user_id, payment_id)`, the kind check widened), owners as the only recipients, a `DepositProofMail` with the dashboard project link and no amount, link, token or reference code (ruling 5), and the job calling `AlertService`. Why: ruling 1. Rejected: building it now.
13. **No "your intake form is at its limit" alert (TAT-38).** 429s and busy 503s stay unlogged and unalerted; `deploy.md`'s "Accepted denial costs" keeps saying so, now naming this ruling. Rejected: an alert on the first refusal per hour (a counter per studio and a new kind, for a cost the pilot hasn't hit).
14. **One-tap links come from the stored phone only.** WhatsApp: `https://wa.me/<digits>?text=<encodeURIComponent(text)>`; Viber: `viber://chat?number=%2B<digits>`, which can't carry text, so the message has a "Copy message" button and the hint "Viber opens the chat empty: copy the message first, then paste it there."; a call: `tel:+<E.164>`. The digits are the client's stored phone (`clients.phone`, E.164 by a database check), through the existing `chatLinks` and `telLink` (`#modules/clients/contact_links`); nothing a person typed ever becomes part of a link's address. The text is percent-encoded as UTF-8, so Devanagari, emoji, newlines (`%0A`), `&`, `#`, `+` and `?` arrive as written. A template is at most 1,000 characters (ruling 20) and placeholder values are short, so a link stays a few kilobytes at worst (Devanagari takes 9 bytes per character encoded), which WhatsApp and the browsers accept. Rejected: building links in the browser from the textarea (ruling 24); `whatsapp://send` (doesn't open WhatsApp Web on a laptop).
15. **Where the links are.** Every project page gets a "Message the client" card, right after the status card: the message chooser (ruling 25), the chosen message with "Send on WhatsApp", "Open Viber chat" and "Copy message", and always, when the client has a phone, plain "WhatsApp", "Viber" and "Call" links without a message ("This client has no phone number to message." for an anonymized client). The quote card keeps its quote message (TAT-64) and the deposit card its deposit-link message (TAT-65, owners only), both now from the studio's templates. A new inquiry's page gets the same card with the "Inquiry received" message (a decided inquiry's card has the plain links only). The inbox's New list shows each inquiry's last contact. The client profile keeps its plain links (TAT-41) with no templates and records nothing (a client spans projects; the contact is a project's). Rejected: chat buttons on every inbox row (four 44 px buttons per row on a phone); templates on the client profile (which project would they name?).
16. **Six kinds, each offered where it makes sense.**

    | Kind | Label | Offered | Placeholders |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `inquiry_received` | Inquiry received | A `new` inquiry's page; a project in `reviewing` | `{client_name}`, `{studio_name}` |
    | `quote` | Quote | The quote card, while the project is `quoted` or `on_hold` with a live quote (TAT-64's rule) | `{client_name}`, `{studio_name}`, `{price}`, `{deposit_note}`, `{valid_until}` |
    | `deposit_link` | Deposit link | The deposit card, owners only, with a working link and a phone (TAT-65's rule) | `{client_name}`, `{studio_name}`, `{amount}`, `{reference_code}`, `{deposit_link}` |
    | `booking_confirmation` | Booking confirmation | A project that isn't declined or cancelled with a scheduled session still to come | `{client_name}`, `{studio_name}`, `{artist_name}`, `{session_time}`, `{studio_address}` |
    | `session_reminder` | Session reminder | The same | the same |
    | `aftercare` | Aftercare | A project that isn't declined or cancelled with a completed tattoo or touch-up session | `{client_name}`, `{studio_name}`, `{artist_name}` |

17. **Default texts: English, plain, the same greeting everywhere.** `quote` and `deposit_link` are TAT-64's and TAT-65's messages exactly (ruling 23). The others: inquiry received "Hi {client_name}, thank you for your tattoo inquiry to {studio_name}. We have your idea and will reply here soon."; booking confirmation "Hi {client_name}, your session at {studio_name} is booked for {session_time} with {artist_name}. Reply here if you need to change it."; session reminder "Hi {client_name}, a reminder of your session at {studio_name} on {session_time} with {artist_name}. See you soon!"; aftercare "Hi {client_name}, thank you for your tattoo at {studio_name}! For the next two weeks: wash it gently with clean water and mild soap and pat it dry, put on a thin layer of aftercare cream, don't scratch or pick it, and keep it out of the sun, pools and the sea. Message us here if anything worries you." Why: short, polite English reads fine to Nepali clients and tourists alike, and a studio that prefers "Namaste" or Nepali changes the text once. Rejected: Nepali defaults (nobody to proofread them); "Namaste" in the new four only (two greetings side by side).
18. **Placeholders: a fixed list per kind, and what a missing value does.** The names are lower-case words in braces (table above). `{client_name}` is the first word of the name; `{artist_name}` is the session's artist (confirmation, reminder) or the project's (aftercare), "your artist" when there is none or the profile was deleted; `{session_time}` is the next session's `sessionTimeText` ("Mon 2 Nov 2026, 15:45–17:45"); `{studio_address}` is the studio profile's address; `{price}`, `{deposit_note}` ("Deposit: NPR 3,000." or "No deposit needed."), `{valid_until}`, `{amount}`, `{reference_code}` and `{deposit_link}` are TAT-64's and TAT-65's values. An empty value removes the placeholder and one space before it, so "Hi {client_name}," reads "Hi," for a client without a name (TAT-64's own behaviour) and an address-less studio's "{studio_address}" disappears. Rejected: conditional sections in templates (a template language for six messages).
19. **Rendering is one pass over plain text.** `renderMessage(kind, template, values)` replaces each `{name}` of the kind's list with its value in a single `String.replace` with a function: a value is never read again, so a client named "{deposit_link}" or "$&" stays exactly that, and a name the kind doesn't know stays as typed. Messages are plain text: no HTML, no Markdown; React prints them as text and the link encodes them. Rejected: a template engine such as Edge (expressions and escaping rules a chat message doesn't need, and a way to run code from a setting).
20. **What a template may be.** On save each text is trimmed, its line endings become `\n`, and then: no NUL character; 1 to 1,000 characters (code points, as the `varchar(1000)` counts them); every `{…}` in it is one of that kind's placeholders, so a typo such as `{client name}` or `{Client_Name}` is a field error rather than a message that shows braces; and the deposit-link message keeps `{deposit_link}`. Each error is shown under its own field and nothing is saved. Messages: "Write the message, or reset it to the default.", "Use at most 1,000 characters.", "Remove the unsupported characters.", "{x} can't be used here. This message can use {client_name}, {studio_name}…", "Keep {deposit_link}: it is the link the client pays on." Rejected: allowing literal braces (a studio wanting `{` in a chat message is rarer than a mistyped placeholder).
21. **Storage: a row only for a changed message.** `message_templates` (tenant-owned, RLS, unique `(tenant_id, kind)`, `body varchar(1000)` not blank). The defaults live in code (`DEFAULT_TEMPLATES`); a studio with no row uses them, and saving a text equal to the default deletes the row, so a later improvement of a default reaches every studio that never changed it. `MessageTemplateService.textsFor(tenant)` gives all six in one query. Not soft-deleted: a setting replaced in place, like deposit links. Rejected: copying the defaults into every studio at signup (a backfill, and defaults frozen at their first version).
22. **The editor: Settings, Messages, owners only.** `/t/:tenant/settings/messages`, a new entry in the settings sidebar. One form with the six messages in lifecycle order; each has its label, where it is offered, a textarea (the saved text), its placeholders with what each means, a live preview with example details (the studio's own name and address; "Sita", "Asha", "NPR 15,000" and so on for the rest) and, when the text differs from the default, "Reset to default", which puts the default text in the box (saving applies it). One "Save messages" button ("Saving…"), the flash "Messages saved.", field errors under each box. One column at 375 px. The preview runs in the browser with `previewMessage` (`inertia/components/messages.ts`), a restatement of the server's renderer that a unit test keeps in step. Rejected: a form per message (Inertia's one error bag would show one form's error under all six); a server-rendered preview (stale while typing).
23. **TAT-64's and TAT-65's messages fold in without changing a byte.** `quoteMessage(input, template = DEFAULT_TEMPLATES.quote)` and `depositMessage(input, template = DEFAULT_TEMPLATES.deposit_link)` keep their names, inputs and callers, and render through `renderMessage`. Their existing unit tests and the project and deposit page tests, unchanged, pin that a studio which never edited them sends exactly what it sent before. The project page passes the studio's own text. `chatLinks` is unchanged. Rejected: new functions beside the old ones (two ways to write the same message).
24. **Staff edit a message in the chat app, not on the page.** WhatsApp opens with the text in its compose box, where it can be changed before sending; Viber gets a pasted copy. The page shows the text read-only. Why: the links stay built on the server from the stored phone (ruling 14). Rejected: an editable textarea that rebuilds the link in the browser.
25. **Which session, and which message first.** The confirmation and the reminder name the project's next scheduled session: the earliest `scheduled` one whose start is still to come, of any kind; with none, neither is offered. Aftercare is offered once a tattoo or touch-up session is `completed`. The chooser (a native select, 44 px, labelled "Message"; a single message shows its label instead) starts on: in `reviewing`, inquiry received; in `booked`, the booking confirmation; otherwise aftercare, then the reminder, then the confirmation, whichever is offered. A project with none of the four shows only the plain links. Rejected: a message button on each session row (TAT-85's rows already carry four buttons and a panel).
26. **Nothing is sent automatically.** No scheduled reminder, no confirmation on booking, no client email or SMS: staff tap the link (the requirements: "No automatic customer messages in the MVP"). TAT-85's carry-forward for reminders stays with TAT-74. Rejected: a reminder job (there is no WhatsApp API to send through).
27. **The last contact: when, on which channel, with which message.** Tapping "Send on WhatsApp", "Open Viber chat", "Copy message" or a plain WhatsApp, Viber or Call link posts, in the background, to the existing `POST /t/:tenant/projects/:id/contacted` (TAT-46, which the chat links already call) or the new `POST /t/:tenant/inbox/:id/contacted`, with an optional `channel` (`whatsapp`, `viber`, `call`, `copy`) and `kind` (a message kind); "Mark contacted" sends neither and records the time only. The project and the inquiry store `last_contacted_at`, `last_contact_channel` and `last_contact_kind`; never the text, the link, a token or the phone. It reads "contacted today on WhatsApp (Quote)", "contacted 2 days ago with a copied message (Deposit link)", "contacted yesterday by phone" or "contacted today" (`lastContactText`). The request is an Inertia visit with `preserveState` (the page refreshes its contact line and shows "Marked as contacted."), as TAT-64 does: no redirect route through our server, so nothing can be made to bounce to another address. Who tapped is not recorded (carry-forward: a `last_contacted_by_user_id`). Rejected: a redirect route that records and then sends the browser to wa.me (an open-redirect shape for a log line); `sendBeacon` (no CSRF header).
28. **Inquiries get their own last contact, and it carries to the project.** `inquiries` gains the same three columns, and `inquiries_guard_update` frees exactly those three (fail closed: everything submitted stays frozen, and the scrub stays one-shot). `InquiryService.markContacted` writes them under the inquiry's row lock, for owners only (the inbox's role check). Accepting the inquiry copies them to the new project (`createFromInquiry`), so "contacted today on WhatsApp (Inquiry received)" is still there on the project page. Rejected: recording an inquiry's contact on the client (a client spans inquiries and projects); not recording it (box 4 would miss the first message).
29. **Who may do what.**

    | Action | Owner | Artist member |
    |---|---|---|
    | Settings, Messages: read and save | yes | 404 (settings group) |
    | Inquiry page's message and its contact record | yes | 404 (inbox group) |
    | Project's messages and its contact record | any project | only a project assigned to their own live profile (`findFor`, then `lockFor` under the lock); others 404 |
    | Deposit-link message | yes (TAT-65) | never shown |
    | Alerts list, open, mark all read | their own alerts | their own alerts |
    | Another member's alert, any studio's | 404 | 404 |

30. **Six new routes, each with an isolation case.** `GET` and `PUT /t/:tenant/settings/messages` (`tenant.settings.messages`, `tenant.settings.messages.update`; the settings group), `POST /t/:tenant/inbox/:id/contacted` (`tenant.inbox.contacted.store`; the inbox group), and a new `/alerts` group with `role({ allow: ["owner", "artist"] })`: `GET /t/:tenant/alerts` (`tenant.alerts.index`), `POST /t/:tenant/alerts/read` (`tenant.alerts.read`), `POST /t/:tenant/alerts/:id/open` (`tenant.alerts.open`). The project contact route keeps its name and case. `tenant_routes.spec.ts` gets the `alerts` area, and `twoStudios` an unread alert for each owner (`alert`) as the open case's resource. The kind of a message travels in the body, never the path: the isolation harness swaps only the slug and `:id`.
31. **Three additive migrations.** (a) `tattoo_projects` and `inquiries` gain `last_contact_channel` and `last_contact_kind` (check-listed values), `inquiries` gains `last_contacted_at`, both get a `<table>_last_contact_needs_time` check, and `inquiries_guard_update` is replaced with the three columns freed (search path still pinned); `down()` restores TAT-37's guard first, then drops the columns. (b) `message_templates` with `enableTenantRls()`. (c) `staff_alerts` (`tenant_id`, `user_id` to `users` on delete cascade, `kind` in (`new_inquiry`), `inquiry_id` by a composite key to `inquiries` on delete cascade, `read_at`, `created_at`; a kind check; unique `(user_id, inquiry_id)`; indexes for the list, the unread count and the key) with `enableTenantRls()`. Both new tables join `EXPECTED_POLICIES`. The value lists are copied into the migrations, not imported. Rolling back loses recorded contacts, studio texts and alerts: fine in development, never in production. Rejected: one migration for everything (the guard replacement deserves its own reviewable file).
32. **Built on the chain tip; conflicts are textual.** The plan quotes every edited file from e2b3c1d. Rejected: nothing else exists to build on.

## The pieces

### `#modules/notifications/messages` (pure, no imports)

`MESSAGE_KINDS`, `MessageKind`, `MESSAGE_LABELS`, `MESSAGE_OFFERED` (the settings page's "where"), `PLACEHOLDERS`, `Placeholder`, `PLACEHOLDER_HELP`, `KIND_PLACEHOLDERS`, `DEFAULT_TEMPLATES`, `MAX_TEMPLATE_LENGTH` (1000), `MessageValues`, `CONTACT_CHANNELS`, `ContactChannel`, `CHANNEL_TEXT`, `ContactVia`, `NO_VIA`; `firstName(name)`, `renderMessage(kind, template, values)`, `normalizeTemplate(text)`, `templateProblem(kind, text)`, `sampleValues(studio)`.

### `#modules/notifications/alerts` (pure)

`ALERT_KINDS` (`new_inquiry`), `AlertKind`, `EXCERPT_LENGTH` (140), `excerpt(text, max?)`, `AlertMember`, `inquiryRecipients(members, preferredUserId)`, `inquiryIntro({ clientName, studioName, artistName, askedForYou })`, `newInquirySubject(studioName)`, `dashboardUrl(appUrl, path)`, `alertCopy(kind, isOwner)`, `alertTimeText(at, timezone, now?)`, `alertTarget(alert, role)`.

### `#modules/notifications/chat` and `project_messages` (pure)

`chatMessage(text, phone)` (`{ text, whatsappUrl, viberUrl }`, TAT-64's shape) and `contactLinks(phone)` (`{ whatsappUrl, viberUrl, callUrl }`). `CARD_KINDS`, `cardKinds({ status, upcomingSession, finishedWork })`, `defaultCardKind(status, kinds)`, `cardValues(kind, facts)`.

| `AlertService.markAllRead(tenant, userId)` | Every unread alert of the member here |
| `TenancyService.find(tenantId)` | The live tenant, or null (a job carries only the id) |
| `TenancyService.members(tenant)` | Live members with their account's email and name, oldest first |
