# Tattoo Drip UX audit (2026-10-19)

Scope: the public studio page, the client deposit page, the dashboard at 375 px (phone) and 1280 px (desktop), and the owner's journeys end to end. Judged against the three complaints: **flow** (confusing), **polish** (plain, unfinished), **feel** (no tattoo-studio character).

How it was run: an isolated copy (database `tattoo_drip_audit`, platform on 3401, storefront on 3404, worker with email switched off), driven with headless Chromium from the repo's Playwright. A throwaway owner signed up through the real form, was confirmed with `account:verify`, created "Inkwell Kathmandu", added 4 artists (one Devanagari name, one closed, one without a photo), 4 payment methods with QR placeholders, a deposit policy, and 10 inquiries sent through the real intake form (3 Devanagari names, 2 tourists, reference photos). Projects were walked through the real UI to reviewing, quoted, deposit pending (link sent, screenshot sent), booked with a session, in progress and cancelled. The deposit page was shot in every state: due, error, screenshot sent, paid, updating, expired, dead link. Screenshots (`ux-shots/<area>-<screen>-<viewport>[-theme].png`, 105 files) were kept outside the repo and are not committed; the "Evidence" names below refer to them.

Not covered: **completed** and **healing check** (no screen or action can reach them yet: see X-2), the artist-member view (invitations aren't built), Turnstile (off here), and load speed (dev servers only, so no honest numbers; see X-12).

## The short version

Most of the engineering underneath is careful: the copy is plain and kind, the forms handle errors well, Devanagari renders correctly everywhere, and the deposit page is solid. The UX problems sit on top of that, in three layers:

1. **Flow.** The app shows its data model, not the studio's day. Screens are fixed stacks of cards, the same whatever the status, so the next step is somewhere down the page. There is no "what needs me now" view. A new owner gets no setup guide and can't find their own studio link.
2. **Polish.** The dashboard still uses the stock shadcn grey theme ("until the shared design system replaces them", `apps/platform/inertia/css/app.css:11`) with a placeholder "TD" logo. Some pages were never finished: the AdonisJS starter home page, raw validation messages, native "Choose File" inputs. All status badges are the same grey. Buttons are 32 to 36 px tall.
3. **Feel.** The studio page has no tattoo photos at all, and its first screen is a name and six identical grey buttons. The dashboard and the studio page look like two different products.

## What is good and must not be lost

- **Copy.** Short, human, specific. Intake errors read "Describe the tattoo you'd like.", "Say where on your body it goes." The dead link says "Ask the studio to send you a new one" and offers WhatsApp and Viber. The "Viber opens the chat empty" hint is honest. Keep this voice: it is already the "calm desk" tone the direction document builds on.
- **Devanagari.** Names and bios render correctly in lists, headings, chat messages and deposit pages (`dir="auto"`, `<bdi>`, no uppercase or letter-spacing on user text). Eczar 700 is already vendored for Latin and Devanagari. Never regress this.
- **The stencil idea.** The violet offset frame behind artist photos, thumbnails and success boxes (`apps/storefront/src/styles.css:184-187`) is a real tattoo reference: transfer stencils are purple. It is the seed of the recommended direction.
- **The deposit page's core.** The amount and the reference code are the largest things on the page, with Copy buttons, the policy, "Tattoo Drip never handles your money", every state worded, and offline and slow-network handling (`deposit-due-fold-phone`, `deposit-submitted-phone`, `deposit-dead-phone`).
- **The intake form's mechanics.** 3 rem controls, one-tap radio chips, a photo tray that prepares images on the phone, an error summary that takes focus, a clear sent state (`studio-intake-errors-phone`, `studio-intake-sent-phone`).
- **Dashboard safety.** Inline confirmations for money and session outcomes ("Mark the session … as done? An outcome can't be changed afterwards", `dash-session-outcome-confirm-phone`), "Saving…" states, toasts after every action, and a proof check that shows the amount asked, the reference code and the method next to the screenshot (`dash-project-proof-to-verify-phone`). Message templates with a live preview (`dash-settings-messages-phone`).
- **Phone-first shell.** A bottom tab bar with badges, safe-area padding, and light and dark themes.
- **Performance discipline.** No third-party scripts, `font-display: optional`, the form loaded in its own chunk, lazy images. Every recommendation below keeps this.

## Studio page (`<slug>.localhost:3404`)

**S-1. No tattoos on a tattoo studio's page.** major, feel. Evidence: `studio-home-phone`, `studio-home-desktop`. The only images are artist portraits; there is no cover image and no work. Clients choose a studio by its work, and the Instagram link sends them away to judge it. Fix: (a) a **cover photo** in Settings → Profile, shown as a full-bleed hero at 4:3 on phones and 21:9 on desktop, with the studio name over a dark scrim; (b) **up to 6 work photos per artist**, a 3-column square grid on the artist page and 3 thumbnails in the roster card, opening a simple lightbox. This needs smaller image sizes first (stored images are full size up to 2048 px, `apps/storefront/docs/development.md` "Photos"). It is a product decision because the roadmap puts the portfolio in V1 (Q2 in the plan).

**S-2. The first screen says little, and contact options are flat.** major, feel and flow. Evidence: `studio-home-fold-phone`. The screen holds the name in 44 to 88 px type, "Tattoo studio", the intro, one violet button and six identical outline chips (Instagram, WhatsApp, Viber, Call, Email, Facebook). In Nepal the real channel is WhatsApp or Viber, and it carries the same weight as Facebook here. Fix: hero = cover photo + name + one line of intro + two buttons: **"Send your idea"** (primary) and **"WhatsApp"** (secondary, with icon). Then group the rest: *Chat* (WhatsApp, Viber, Call), *Follow* (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), *Visit* (address, map link, opening hours once they exist). Replace the generic "Tattoo studio" line with "Thamel, Kathmandu · Custom and walk-in" built from the address and, later, a style field.

**S-3. Desktop wastes half the page.** major, polish. Evidence: `studio-home-desktop`, `studio-artist-desktop`. The form is one 36 rem column on the left and the right half is empty for about 1,000 px. Fix: from 60 rem, put the form in the left 7/12 and a **sticky "How booking works" panel** in the right 5/12: 1. Send your idea and photos. 2. {Studio} replies on WhatsApp with a price. 3. A deposit holds your date. 4. Your session. Add the deposit policy summary and the contact links. On phones, show the same 4 steps as a compact strip above the form.

**S-4. The form opens with a Cloudflare privacy paragraph.** minor, flow and feel. Evidence: `studio-home-phone` (the grey 5-line block before "Your tattoo"), `apps/storefront/src/intake/copy.ts:175`. The first thing a nervous client reads is about spam bots and Cloudflare. Fix: one reassuring line at the top ("Only {studio} sees what you send.") and move the Cloudflare sentence and its link to small print under Send.

**S-5. The form is long, with one question that serves the studio, not the client.** minor, flow. Evidence: `studio-intake-filled-phone` (4,663 px tall at 375 px including the page above). There are 11 questions, and "How did you find {studio}?" is required (`packages/types/openapi.yaml:795`). Fix: make the source optional (a contract change), and number the three groups ("1 Your tattoo · 2 When and who · 3 How we reach you") so the length feels like progress. Keep everything else; the field set matches the requirements.

**S-6. Success is a dead end for excitement.** minor, feel. Evidence: `studio-intake-sent-phone`. It is clear, but flat. Fix: keep the copy and add the 4-step "what happens next" with step 1 ticked, plus "Usually replies within {n} hours" once the studio can set that.

**S-7. Artist presentation.** minor, feel. Evidence: `studio-home-phone`, `studio-artist-phone`. Portraits are 7 rem thumbnails, "Taking new inquiries" is violet text, and a photo that hasn't loaded is a flat lavender block. Fix: on phones, a 2-column card grid (4:5 photo, name, styles, open or closed badge); on the artist page, the photo plus the work grid (S-1); a blurred or tinted placeholder while photos load. The closed-artist page is good (`studio-artist-closed-phone`): keep the message and the "general inquiry" link.

**S-8. The palette is cool lavender-grey.** minor, feel. Evidence: `studio-home-phone`. `--paper: #f5f6f8` with violet reads as a tidy SaaS form more than a studio. Fix: warm the paper and ink per the recommended direction (`ux-direction.md`, C). The tokens already exist, so this is a small change.

**S-9. The dev-only TanStack devtools button** shows in every storefront screenshot (the palm-tree circle). It is not a product issue; make sure it stays dev-only.

## Client deposit page (`/deposit#<token>`)

**D-1. The heading ignores the state.** major, flow. Evidence: `deposit-paid-phone`, `deposit-expired-phone`, `deposit-updating-phone`. All of them read "Pay your deposit to Inkwell Kathmandu" (`apps/storefront/src/deposit/copy.ts:105`) above "Your deposit is paid" or "This quote has expired". Fix: one heading per state: "Deposit paid. You're booked in." / "Screenshot sent" / "Your quote is being updated" / "This quote has expired" / "This booking was cancelled".

**D-2. "Paid" is a dead end.** major, flow and feel. Evidence: `deposit-paid-phone`. One sentence and two links. This is the moment the client is happiest and most likely to share. Fix: make it a confirmation ticket: the amount and reference (a receipt), the **session date and time and the artist** when one is scheduled, the address with a map link, "What to bring / how to prepare" from a studio-written note (later), and "Message {studio}". The session time needs an API field (contract change, plan WP-14).

**D-3. Three full-width QR codes stacked.** major, flow. Evidence: `deposit-due-phone` (3,414 px at 375 px; each QR is about 280 px plus its details). The client scrolls past every method to reach the upload, and then answers "How did you pay?" again (`deposit-error-noscreenshot-phone`). Fix: a **"Pay with" segmented control** (Fonepay | eSewa | Khalti | Bank) showing one method at a time, remembered as the answer to "How did you pay?". Number the steps on the page: **1 Copy the code → 2 Pay → 3 Send the screenshot**, with a sticky "I've paid: send screenshot" button that jumps to step 3.

**D-4. Little studio identity at the point of paying.** minor, feel and trust. Evidence: `deposit-due-fold-phone`. There is no logo, cover or artist photo; the studio name appears only in the heading. Fix: a small header with the studio's cover or logo, name and artist avatar; move "You pay {studio} directly. Tattoo Drip never handles your money." from the footer to just under the amount.

**D-5. Desktop is a phone column.** minor. Evidence: `deposit-due-desktop`. On a computer the client scans the QR with a phone, so the QR is the main act. Fix: from 60 rem, steps and amount on the left, the selected QR large on the right.

**D-6. The other states are good** (`deposit-submitted-phone`, `deposit-dead-phone`): they name the next step and give chat links. Keep them, and give them the same stencil "ticket" frame as the success states.

## Dashboard on a phone (375 px)

**P-1. The project page is a fixed stack; the next step is buried.** blocker, flow. Evidence: `dash-project-after-accept-phone` (3,985 px), `dash-project-deposit-link-phone` (4,747 px), `dash-project-booked-phone-light` (3,609 px); order in `apps/platform/inertia/pages/projects/show.tsx:118-288`. Every project shows Status, Message the client, Sessions, Request, Quote, Deposit, Payments (with the full "Record payment" form always open), Notes, "Issue a quote" or "Revise the quote" (always open, at the very bottom) and Earlier versions. For a project under review, the one thing to do, issue a quote, is the last card, about 4 screens down. After quoting, "send the quote on WhatsApp" and "Client accepted" sit in the Quote card below Sessions and Request. Fix (plan WP-5, WP-11):
- **Header:** client name, a short piece title ("Koi, left calf", from the request), the artist, and a coloured status with a 5-step progress line (Review → Quote → Deposit → Sessions → Done).
- **"Next step" card** directly under the header, chosen by status: reviewing → "Send a quote" (the quote form inline); quoted → "Send the quote on WhatsApp" + "Client accepted"; deposit pending → "Send the deposit link" or "Check the screenshot"; booked → next session + "Send booking confirmation"; in progress → "Schedule the next session" / "Mark done"; done → healing check. The server already computes `canQuote`, `canAccept`, `deposit`, `payments.outcome.needed` and `bookedWithoutSession`, so this is mostly re-ordering.
- **Below:** Sessions, Money (one summary line: "Paid 5,000 of 20,000 · Balance 15,000", the ledger behind "Show payments"), the request with references, notes. "Record payment", "Record a refund" and "Revise the quote" become buttons that open a bottom sheet.
- Status moves (Put on hold, Client withdrew, Decline) go into a "⋯" menu: they are rare and destructive, and today they sit in the first card at the same weight as everything else (`dash-project-quoted-phone`).

**P-2. The inquiry page asks you to decide before you read.** major, flow. Evidence: `dash-inquiry-fold-phone-light`, `dash-inquiry-phone-light`; `apps/platform/inertia/pages/inquiries/show.tsx:67-140`. The "Decide" card comes first, with a 36 px native select squeezed beside the button so it shows only "Aarav ▾"; the idea comes next, and the references, the most telling thing for an artist, come last at 92 px. The phone number wraps mid-digit ("+977984100010 / 1") because of `break-words` (`:114`). Fix: order idea → references (large, swipeable) → details → message; a **sticky bottom action bar** (above the tab bar) with "Accept…" and "Decline", where Accept opens a sheet to pick the artist (full width, 44 px, preselected with the client's choice); phone numbers as `tel:` links with `whitespace-nowrap`; Decline with an inline confirm like the payment panels, not `window.confirm`.

**P-3. Message overload.** major, flow and polish. Evidence: `dash-inquiry-phone-light` (the Message card), `dash-project-booked-phone-light`. Every inquiry and project shows "Send on WhatsApp", "Open Viber chat", "Copy message", the Viber caveat, then "Or open a chat without a message: WhatsApp, Viber, Call", plus "never contacted · Mark contacted" in the status card. Fix: one primary **"Send on WhatsApp"** button with the message preview folded to two lines ("Show message"), a secondary "Other ways" row (Viber + copy, Call, Copy text), and the client's last-used channel remembered as the primary. Put the message inside the Next step card when it is the next step (quote, deposit link, booking confirmation), so there is one place to send.

**P-4. The projects list doesn't say what's waiting.** major, flow. Evidence: `dash-projects-phone`. Rows show client name, artist and "never contacted". There is no piece, no next session date, no money due, and nothing about whose move it is. The status chips (9 with counts) wrap into 4 rows and take a third of the screen. Fix: one horizontally scrolling chip row (Active, Needs you, Reviewing, …); rows show piece title and reference thumbnail, the coloured status, then one "waiting" line ("Quote sent 2 days ago · expires in 12 days", "Screenshot to check", "Next session Sat 24 Oct, 11:00", "Balance NPR 15,000"). Drop "never contacted" from the list.

**P-5. Inbox rows have no picture.** minor, flow and feel. Evidence: `dash-inbox-phone-light`. "1 reference" is text. Fix: a 56 px thumbnail of the first reference on the right of each row; the preferred artist as a small avatar.

**P-6. Artists are hidden.** major, flow. Evidence: `dash-settings-home-phone`, `dash-artists-phone`. Artists live under Settings as a 4th tab that is off-screen at 375 px (the tab row scrolls), and on the artists page no Settings tab is highlighted. For a studio, artists are core. Fix: see the navigation change in X-1 ("More" holds Clients, Artists and Settings as big rows); artist cards with the photo larger, the public link with Copy and Share, and the open or closed state as a switch on the card.

**P-7. Forms still look like raw HTML in places.** minor, polish. Evidence: `dash-artist-form-phone`, `dash-payment-method-form-phone`. Native "Choose File No file chosen" for the photo and QR, with no preview; the artist form's "Link" field is unexplained. Fix: an "Add photo" tile with preview (as the storefront's photo tray does), "Page link: inkwell-kathmandu…/artists/__" with the prefix shown.

**P-8. Devanagari names get no link.** minor, flow. Evidence: `dash-artists-phone` ("सुजना तामाङ /artist-2": the slug came out empty and had to be invented). `slugify` drops non-Latin letters. Fix: transliterate Devanagari to Latin for the suggestion (a small table: सुजना तामाङ → sujana-tamang) and say so under the field.

**P-9. The Messages settings page is 3,900 px of forms.** minor, polish. Evidence: `dash-settings-messages-phone`. Six always-open textareas with `{client_name}` tokens in monospace braces. Fix: a list of six messages (name + first line + Edit); the editor opens one at a time with placeholder chips you tap to insert, and the preview stays.

**P-10. Calendar and alerts are clean but thin.** minor. Evidence: `dash-calendar-phone-light`, `dash-calendar-week-phone-light`, `dash-alerts-filled-phone-light`. The day view is good (time, client, kind, "Now" line). The artist filter needs a separate "Show" tap (a form submit); make it apply on change. Alerts: see X-1.

**P-11. Badges are counted twice.** minor, polish. Evidence: `dash-alerts-filled-phone-light`. The bell shows 3 and the Inbox tab shows 3 for the same new inquiries. Fix: the bell counts things the tabs don't (screenshots to check, quote expiring, session today), or it goes away once Today exists.

## The owner's journeys, walked

**Set up (sign up → confirm → studio → ready).** Signup is clean apart from raw error text (X-4). "Check your email" is clear. Onboarding is good ("Set up your business", studio vs independent, address, time zone), but "Your address" reads like a street address: call it "Your page link". After Create, the owner lands in an empty inbox with no next step (X-3). Adding artists means finding Settings → scrolling the tabs → Artists (P-6). Payment QR codes are under Settings → Payments, with good copy. Nowhere tells the owner the setup is done, or where the page is.
