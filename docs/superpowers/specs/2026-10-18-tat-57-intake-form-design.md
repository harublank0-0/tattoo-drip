# Intake form on the studio page: browser to API, multipart, bot challenge (TAT-57)

Date: 2026-10-18
Refs: TAT-57. Builds on TAT-48 (the `#inquiry` mount point, `InquiryState`, `InquiryChannels`, `SiteRef`, `src/studio/links.ts`, the stencil tokens), TAT-55 (Turnstile, the SDK's `challengeToken` sent as `X-Challenge-Token`, `honeypot` sent as `website`, `INQUIRY_CHALLENGE_ACTION`, `INQUIRY_HONEYPOT_FIELD`, 403/503/429), TAT-51 (`inquiries.create`, fields, limits, codes, `useCreateInquiry` with `retry: false`), TAT-44 (`Artist.id`), TAT-23 (the browser client: no key, no cookies; router dehydrate/hydrate), TAT-12 (`parseStorefrontConfig`, the `node:test` runner). Blocks TAT-80. Linear has no comments on TAT-57 (checked 2026-10-18). Written without the user (unattended run): every open question is a ruling below.

## Goal

A person on a phone, often on a slow connection in Nepal, sends a studio their idea, up to 5 reference photos and a WhatsApp or Viber number straight to the API, passes a bot check they rarely see, and is told the studio will reply on WhatsApp or Viber. Photos are shrunk in the browser first. Storefront and docs only: no platform, contract, SDK or React change, no dependency change, one new setting (`TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY`).

## Base

Designed on c103fa0 (TAT-55's design commit; only the designs of TAT-44, 48 and 55 are in it). ## Rulings

1. **Scope: the form; the contract stays.** `useCreateInquiry()` as TAT-51/55 built it (no `signal`, the SDK's 120 s upload timeout, `retry: false`). Rejected: upload progress or an abort option in the SDK or hook (ruling 7).
2. **Mounted only in the browser, in its own chunk.** In `open`, `InquirySection` renders `IntakeMount`: on the server, before hydration, without JavaScript or when the chunk fails, TAT-48's WhatsApp/email fallback exactly as it is; after hydration the form, loaded with `React.lazy`. The closed states are unchanged. Why: on a slow phone, text typed into server-rendered inputs before hydration is lost or out of sync with React, and a native submit before hydration would put the answers in a GET URL; the first load stays as light as TAT-48's; without JavaScript the chat links still work. Rejected: a server-rendered form, live or disabled until hydration (a dead form for no-JS browsers).
3. **The contract's fields, nothing more.** "Your tattoo": idea, reference photos, placement, rough size, preferred dates, artist, budget, how they found the studio. "How <Studio> reaches you": name, WhatsApp or Viber number, email. Required unless the label says "(optional)" (photos, budget, email). The artist choice (radios, "No preference" first) shows only when 2 or more artists accept, or on an artist's page (that artist preselected, so they can still switch); otherwise no `preferredArtistId` is sent. How they found the studio: six radio chips, none preselected. Rejected: selects (Radix popovers on phones; a radio is one visible tap); a style field (V1's style list).
4. **Validation mirrors the contract; the API decides.** A pure `validateIntake`: trimmed required fields, the column lengths (pinned to `openapi.yaml` by a test; `maxLength` on the one-line fields only, so a long idea pasted into its box is never cut silently and gets the length error instead), no NUL, a loose email shape, the phone through a copy of the platform's `normalizePhone` (pinned by the platform's own table) with the platform's message. Devanagari digits (a Nepali keyboard's `९८१२…`) become ASCII digits before the check and in what is sent. Values go trimmed; the phone otherwise as typed (the API stores E.164). Validation runs on submit, then on change. Rejected: Zod (more JavaScript on slow phones for seven rules); sending a normalized phone (the contract takes it as typed); a minimum idea length.
5. **Photos are re-encoded in the browser, one at a time.** Picker: `accept="image/jpeg,image/png,image/webp"`, `multiple` (iOS then converts HEIC to JPEG). Each photo is decoded with `createImageBitmap(file, { imageOrientation: "from-image" })`, else through an `<img>`; drawn on white (transparent PNGs); long edge at most 1600 px (never enlarged); JPEG at 0.85, then 0.72 if over 1 MiB, then 1280 px at 0.72; over 3.5 MiB after that it is refused, so 5 photos stay under the API's 20 MB. What is sent is always the re-encoded `reference.jpg`: no EXIF, no GPS, no file name. Sources over 50 MiB, other types and files the browser can't decode are refused with a message (a HEIC it can't decode: "add a screenshot of it or a JPEG copy"). Five is a hard cap: extra picks aren't added, and a message says how many. One decode at a time on the main thread; bitmaps are closed and canvases emptied after each; previews are object URLs of the small JPEGs, revoked on remove, on success and on unmount. `prepareReference` is pure behind an `ImageCodec` seam. Rejected: 2048 px (about twice the bytes on a slow upload for detail the inbox doesn't show); WebP output (Safari's canvas can't encode it); a worker or `OffscreenCanvas` (uneven support and a second bundle to save 0.2 to 1 s per photo); a WASM HEIC decoder (megabytes on slow phones, and a dependency); sending the original when re-encoding fails.
6. **Turnstile: loaded late, run per attempt.** The script (`https://challenges.cloudflare.com/turnstile/v0/api.js?render=explicit&onload=…`) loads on the first focus, tap or key in the form, or at submit; never on page view. It renders into a box above Send with `action: INQUIRY_CHALLENGE_ACTION`, `execution: "execute"`, `appearance: "interaction-only"`, `size: "flexible"`, `theme: "auto"`, `retry: "never"`, `refresh-expired: "manual"`, `response-field: false`, `feedback-enabled: false`. Send calls `execute` and waits for the token (20 s for the script, 30 s for an answer, no limit while the checkbox is showing), and the widget is reset after every attempt, whatever the answer (tokens are single-use). A script that never loads, a widget error, a timed-out checkbox and an unsupported browser each have a message, with the studio's chat links. `Challenge` is a class behind a `ChallengeHost` seam. Rejected: loading on page view or near the viewport (Cloudflare would see every visitor); running at render (a token lives 300 s, a form takes longer); Cloudflare's automatic retry (we'd show a failure it is still retrying); an npm wrapper (a dependency).
7. **Sending: honest waiting, no progress bar, no cancel.** `fetch` reports no upload progress and the SDK stays as it is, so the status line says what is happening: "Preparing your photos…" (Send waits for them), "Running a quick security check…", "Tick the box above to send.", "Sending your inquiry with 3 photos (2.1 MB)…", and after 15 s "Still sending. On a slow connection this can take up to 2 minutes; keep this page open.", over an indeterminate line. The SDK's 120 s timeout stands. No cancel: the hook passes no `signal`, and a request stopped after its body went out may still create the inquiry, so "Stop" could promise nothing; leaving the page is the way out, and a `beforeunload` prompt asks first while sending. Rejected: XHR progress or a `signal` through the SDK and hook (a contract change; and the SDK combines signals with `AbortSignal.any`, missing before iOS 17.4, where every signalled send would fail as `network_error`); a longer timeout (the API holds a pooled database connection for the whole upload, TAT-51/55).
8. **One attempt at a time; errors lose nothing.** `createSubmitter` answers `busy` to a second call while one runs, and Send is `aria-disabled` (it keeps focus) while busy. An attempt validates, waits for the photos, takes a token, sends once and resets the widget. Every outcome but success leaves each field and photo as it was; "Send again" reuses them with a new token.
9. **`TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY`: public, carried like the API connection.** Only `parseStorefrontConfig` reads it (`[A-Za-z0-9_-]{10,100}`, never echoed). Required when `NODE_ENV=production`: without it the settings error makes every page a 500, as for `API_URL`, which beats a live form whose every send is `challenge_failed`. Optional elsewhere: no widget and no token, which the platform accepts while its own secret is unset. The server puts it in `SiteRef` (`{ site: { origin, challengeSiteKey } }`), the browser takes it from the router's dehydrated state, and the two routes pass it to the page components. Never `VITE_` (inlined at build). The client-bundle check also forbids the names `TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` and `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY`; `check:page` runs with Cloudflare's always-pass test key and checks the page carries it (it is public by design) while the server key stays out. Rejected: loader data (one more copy per page); a second set-once ref.
10. **The honeypot, as TAT-55 asks.** A text input `name="website"` (`INQUIRY_HONEYPOT_FIELD`) in an off-screen, `aria-hidden` wrapper (not `display: none`), `tabindex="-1"`, `autocomplete="off"`, label "Leave this empty", uncontrolled. Its DOM value goes as `honeypot`; the form never writes it.
11. **Errors: fixed copy, on the field when there is one, always a way on.** `describeSubmitError` (table in the plan): `validation_failed` becomes field errors (the API's field messages, shown as text; `referenceImages.<i>` on that photo, `referenceImages` on the tray; nothing it can place: "Some answers weren't accepted"); `artist_not_accepting` an error on the artist choice; `too_many_open_inquiries`, `studio_not_accepting`, `challenge_failed`, `challenge_unavailable`, `rate_limited` (Send blocked until `Retry-After` passes, said in minutes or hours), 413 (with or without the envelope), 400, 404 (reload), `timeout`, `network_error` and everything else (5xx, 415, `invalid_response`, unknown errors) become notices: a title, a body, "Send again" where trying again can help, the studio's chat links where the form can't. `error.message` is never shown. A refused send shows an error summary (focused, linking to each field) or a notice (focused); field messages are tied with `aria-describedby`, and only the summary or the notice is announced.
12. **Success.** The form gives way to "Inquiry sent": "<Studio> will reply on WhatsApp or Viber at <the number as typed>.", no promise about time, the chat links to add something, and "Back to the studio page" (`/`). Values, photos and object URLs are cleared and focus goes to the heading; the URL doesn't change and holds nothing about the person. No "Send another" (each copy uses one of the phone's three open slots; a reload starts over).
13. **No drafts, no analytics.** State lives in the page's memory only: no local or session storage (shared phones), no events. A privacy line opens the form: only the studio sees what is sent, Tattoo Drip runs the page, and Cloudflare's check starts when you begin filling it in and sees your connection and device, not your answers (linking Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum). Rejected: a session-storage draft.
14. **All copy in one module, English first.** `src/intake/copy.ts` holds every string as a template with named placeholders (`{studio}`, `{phone}`…); `fill` makes text and `<Fill>` renders the values in `<bdi>`. A Nepali file of the same shape is later work (TAT-80 or after). Rejected: an i18n library.
15. **Accessibility and layout.** Labels, legends, hints through `aria-describedby`, `required` and `aria-required`, "(optional)" in labels, `autocomplete` (`name`, `tel`, `email`), `inputmode`, `type="tel"` and `type="email"`. Controls 3rem tall with 1.0625rem text (no iOS zoom), choices and remove buttons 2.75rem; one column at 375 px, placement and size side by side from 40rem; TAT-48's focus ring; `aria-live` for the status line and photo messages; reduced motion stops the sending line. The toolkit (`src/components/ui/form.tsx`) takes these sizes, gains a native-radio `ChoiceField`, stops announcing every field error (`role="alert"`) and imports by relative path, so node tests can load it.
16. **The stencil carries on.** Thumbnails and the success block take the artist photo's violet offset frame; choices look like TAT-48's link chips with the native radio kept visible (violet accent) and a violet wash when checked; the one motion is the sending line, which answers the tap; red is for errors only.
17. **No CSP yet; what one must allow.** The app sends none today. A future one needs `script-src` and `frame-src https://challenges.cloudflare.com`, `connect-src` with `API_URL`, `img-src blob:` (previews) plus the API's photo origin.
18. **The prototype goes.** `src/features/intake/` (TAT-48's unmounted booking form and schema) is deleted, the development guide names the intake form as the forms reference, and TAT-48's guard test bans `features/`.
19. **Tests, and what only a browser can show.** `node:test` + `tsx` in `test/intake/`, through seams: `ImageCodec` (a fake decode and encode with chosen sizes), `ChallengeHost` (a fake script and `turnstile`), `send`; the SDK's real `inquiries.create` with a fake `fetch` for the wire; `renderToStaticMarkup` for markup. The real codec, the real widget, iOS's HEIC conversion, EXIF orientation, memory on a low-end Android phone and a slow network are the plan's browser pass (the user's, if no browser tool is available).
20. **Requirements.** The three Intake boxes and the studio-page box TAT-48 left open are ticked; the studio-page rule's "will be the one exception" becomes "is the one exception".

## UI

TAT-48's tokens, type and focus ring; nothing new but `--destructive`, for errors only. Body text 1.0625rem; labels 1rem semibold; hints and notes 0.9375rem ink-muted; legends in Eczar 700 at 1.5rem (h3 size) without being headings, so TAT-48's heading order holds. Controls: 3rem tall, 1px `line-strong` border, radius 0.25rem; the idea box 8rem tall and growing. Choices: chips of at least 2.75rem with the radio inside. Photos: 6rem squares in a wrapping tray, each with the violet offset frame, a 2.75rem remove button in its corner and "Preparing…" until ready; the add tile is a dashed violet square with "Add photos" and a count line under the tray ("2 of 5 photos, 1.4 MB in total"). Send is TAT-48's `action-primary`, full width under 40rem. The sending line: 2px violet, sliding under Send, removed under reduced motion. Error summary and notices: a 2px red rule on the start edge, a bold title, links in violet. Success: the block with the violet frame, Eczar heading "Inquiry sent".
