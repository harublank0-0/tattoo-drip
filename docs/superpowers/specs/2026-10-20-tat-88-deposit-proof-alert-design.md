# Staff alert when a client uploads deposit proof (TAT-88)

Date: 2026-10-20
Refs: TAT-88 (Staff alert when a client uploads deposit proof). Left over from TAT-65 ("Owners are alerted that proof has arrived") and TAT-52 (ruling 12, which declined it and described this follow-up). Builds on TAT-52 (`staff_alerts`, `AlertService`, `inquiryRecipients`, `sendMailInTransaction`, `ALERT_EMAILS`, the Alerts page and the bell), TAT-65 (`DepositPageService.submitProof`, `DepositProofAlertJob`), TAT-66 (the project page's Payments card, `id="payments"`) and TAT-37 (`payments_guard`: a proof can never be replaced). TAT-102 (alerts that say who and what) and TAT-107 (Today, the bell) come after it and touch the same code.
Size: **about 1.5 points** (the ticket guesses 1). The alert itself is TAT-52's pattern again; the half point is the constraint swap on `staff_alerts`, a second email, and opening an alert at the Payments card, which needs a location visit because an Inertia redirect drops the `#payments` fragment (ruling 9).

## Goal

When a client sends a deposit screenshot from the deposit page, the studio's owners, and the member who is the project's artist, hear about it once: an alert under the dashboard's bell and an email from the worker. Opening either lands on the project's Payments card, where an owner verifies or rejects the screenshot.

One additive migration (two columns, a key and two replaced checks on `staff_alerts`), no new setting (`ALERT_EMAILS` covers this email too), no new route, no API, OpenAPI, SDK or storefront change. Written without the user (unattended run): every open question is a ruling below.

## Depends on the built code

Designed on 65eab5f (TAT-98's tip), which holds TAT-52's notifications module, TAT-65's job, TAT-66's Payments card and TAT-18's migrations (the last is `1791680000002_create_account_tokens_table.ts`). The plan's Task 0 checks the named code it edits, not a commit.

## Rulings

1. **About 1.5 points: the four required boxes, nothing more.** In: the `deposit_proof` kind and its columns; the alert made by `DepositProofAlertJob` through `AlertService` with the new-inquiry rule for recipients; the email; the bell and the Alerts page; opening at the Payments card; `ALERT_EMAILS`; once per member per payment; tests including the rollbacks. Out: the optional intake-limit alert (ruling 12), an alert to staff when an owner verifies or rejects (nobody asked), per-member email preferences and pruning (TAT-52's carry-forwards, unchanged). Why: each item in is one of the ticket's boxes. Rejected: the optional box (not cheap, ruling 12).
2. **The job makes the alert; the job itself is queued in the payment's transaction.** `DepositPageService.submitProof` keeps queuing `DepositProofAlertJob` with `dispatchInTransaction` in the payment's transaction (TAT-65, unchanged), so the job exists exactly when the payment does; the job now calls `AlertService.depositProof`, which inserts the alerts and queues their emails together in one transaction. That is the box's "same transaction as the submitted payment" in the form that matters: no payment, no alert (TAT-65's existing test "a failure later in the request deletes the image and drops the alert"), and an alert never without its email or the reverse (a new rollback test). It is also the new-inquiry pattern the ticket asks for. Why not in the request: the deposit page is a guest's request through a token; inserting staff alerts there would read every member's email address and queue their emails inside the client's upload, so a bug or a mail hiccup in alerting would refuse the client's screenshot. Rejected: inserting the alerts in `submitProof`'s transaction (that coupling, for an alert the bell only shows on the next page load anyway).
3. **Who is alerted: the new-inquiry rule, with the project's artist in place of the asked-for one.** Every live owner membership of the studio, plus the member linked to the project's artist when, at the time the job runs, the project has an artist, that profile is live, linked to a user (`artists.user_id`), and the user has a live membership in the studio (any role). One alert per user: an owner who is the project's artist gets one, the owner's version, which says it is their project. Removed members, unlinked or deleted profiles, a profile linked to someone outside the studio, and other studios' members get nothing. `inquiryRecipients(members, userId)` (`#modules/notifications/alerts`) is reused unchanged: its rule is exactly this one, and its `askedForYou` flag reads here as "it is your project". Rejected: owners only (TAT-52 ruling 12 proposed it; the ticket asks for the artist, who sees the project's payment list and wants to know the client paid); every member (artists of other projects can't open this one).
4. **Exactly one alert and one email per member per payment.** `staff_alerts` gets a unique `(user_id, payment_id)`; the insert is TAT-52's `insert … on conflict do nothing returning user_id`, and emails are queued only for the rows inserted now, in the same transaction. A retried job, a rerun after a crash or two workers at once add nothing. A second screenshot on the same payment can't happen: the deposit page takes one screenshot at a time (state `submitted` refuses another) and `payments_guard` never lets a payment's proof be replaced. A client's next screenshot is therefore always a new payment, possible only after the studio rejected the last one, and it alerts again: it is a new thing to check. Rejected: a key on `(user_id, project_id)` (a resubmission after a rejection would never reach anyone).
5. **When nobody is alerted.** The job keeps TAT-65's check and log line ("Deposit proof received." with the tenant, project and payment ids) and then alerts nobody when the payment is gone or no longer `submitted` (an owner verified or rejected it at the desk before the worker ran), when the project is deleted (`ProjectService.findLive` returns null), or when the tenant is gone (`AlertService` checks it, as for inquiries). A deleted client still alerts, as "A client".
6. **Schema: the payment and its project on the alert, by one composite key.** Migration `1791694000000_add_deposit_proof_to_staff_alerts.ts`: `project_id uuid null` and `payment_id uuid null`; `staff_alerts_payment_foreign` = `(tenant_id, project_id, payment_id)` references `payments (tenant_id, project_id, id)` `on delete cascade` (the target is TAT-66's `payments_tenant_project_id_unique`), so the payment is this studio's and belongs to that project; `staff_alerts_kind_check` replaced to allow `new_inquiry` and `deposit_proof`; `staff_alerts_subject_matches_kind` replaced by "a new inquiry names an inquiry and no payment, a deposit proof names a payment and no inquiry, and a payment always comes with its project": `(kind = 'new_inquiry') = (inquiry_id is not null) and (kind = 'deposit_proof') = (payment_id is not null) and (project_id is null) = (payment_id is null)`; `staff_alerts_user_id_payment_id_unique`; an index on `(tenant_id, project_id, payment_id)` for the key. Postgres unique keys treat nulls as distinct, so new-inquiry rows never collide on the payment key and deposit-proof rows never on `(user_id, inquiry_id)`. Payments are never deleted (`payments_guard`), so the cascade only matters for a tenant purge, which that guard already makes an explicit operations step. `down()` deletes the deposit-proof alerts first (the old checks can't hold them), then restores TAT-52's checks and drops the columns. The kinds are copied into the migration, not imported. Row-level security already covers the table (`EXPECTED_POLICIES` unchanged). Rejected: `payment_id` alone (opening the alert, and TAT-102's detail, would read the payment first, and nothing would prove the two agree); a table per kind (the list, the count and "Mark all as read" read one table).
7. **What the email may contain.** `DepositProofMail` (`#modules/notifications/emails/deposit_proof_mail`, templates `notifications::emails/deposit_proof` and `deposit_proof_text`). Subject "Deposit screenshot for {studio}" (no client data: `SendMailJob.failed()` logs subjects). Title "Deposit screenshot", preheader "A deposit screenshot is waiting to be checked.". "Hi {recipient's name, or there},", the intro (`proofIntro`): "Sita sent Black Needle a deposit screenshot." or, for the project's artist, "… a deposit screenshot for your project."; "A client sent …" without a name; the first word of the project's client's name as stored (Devanagari stays as typed: `firstName` splits on whitespace only). Owners then read "Check that the money reached your account, then verify or reject it on the Payments card." and the button "Check the screenshot"; the project's artist (not an owner) reads "An owner of {studio} will check it." and the button "Open the project". Both buttons are `APP_URL` + `/t/<slug>/projects/<id>#payments` (a signed-in member's page: the project page 404s anyone else). Then "You get these emails as a member of {studio} on Tattoo Drip." The text version says the same with the link written out. Never: the amount, the payment method, the reference code, the deposit link or its token, the phone, the screenshot or any URL to it, a last name (TAT-52 rulings 5 and 12 kept). Rejected: the amount in the email (an inbox keeps it forever, and the owner sees it one tap away next to the screenshot).
8. **The in-app lines stay fixed per kind.** Owners: "Deposit screenshot to check" / "Open the project's Payments card."; anyone else (the project's artist): "A deposit screenshot for your project" / "An owner checks it on the Payments card.". The row holds ids only (member, kind, project, payment), so TAT-42's privacy deletion has nothing to scrub here. The empty list now reads "No alerts yet. New inquiries and deposit screenshots show up here." TAT-102 replaces the detail with who and what; for this kind that is the client's first name and the amount ("Sita: NPR 3,000"), read when the list renders from `project_id` (its client) and `payment_id` (its amount, formatted with `formatMoney`), never stored on the row and never a phone. The columns of ruling 6 are what it needs. Rejected: copying the name or amount onto the row (a second copy of client data to scrub and keep in step).
9. **Opening the alert lands on the Payments card.** `alertTarget` gives a deposit-proof alert with a project `{ route: "tenant.projects.show", id: projectId, hash: "payments" }` for any role (an owner checks it there; the project's artist sees the payment list there). `AlertsController.open` marks the alert read as before, then: for an Inertia visit (the Alerts page's buttons) it answers `ctx.inertia.location("/t/<slug>/projects/<id>#payments")`, a 409 with `X-Inertia-Location` that makes the browser load that URL, fragment included, so Inertia scrolls to the card's existing `id="payments"`; for a plain request it redirects (302) to the same URL. Why: an Inertia visit follows a redirect by XHR and keeps only the request's own fragment (Inertia 3's `pageUrl`), and @adonisjs/inertia 5 has no `preserveFragment`, so a plain redirect would open the project page at the top, seven cards above Payments on a phone. The path is built from the route name and a constant, never from the request (no open redirect). An artist whose project was reassigned after the alert gets the project page's usual 404. New-inquiry alerts open exactly as before. Rejected: a `?focus=payments` query the project page would read (client code on the page TAT-108 restructures); landing at the top of the page.
10. **The same switch for email.** `ALERT_EMAILS=false` keeps the deposit-proof alerts in the dashboard and queues no email, exactly as for inquiries; the boot warning becomes "Staff alert emails are off (ALERT_EMAILS=false): new inquiries and deposit screenshots alert the dashboard only." Production still needs the sending domain (TAT-20) before turning it on.
11. **The Projects badge and the "Screenshot to check" flag stay.** They count work left; the bell counts alerts. Verifying or rejecting the payment doesn't mark its alert read, and opening the alert doesn't verify anything (TAT-52 ruling 10). TAT-107 decides what the bell counts once Today lists screenshots to check.
12. **No intake-limit alert (the optional box, declined).** The open-inquiry limit is per phone: `IntakeService.checkOpenLimit` refuses one number's fourth open inquiry, which is the client's own repeat request, not the studio being full, and it throws inside the inquiry's transaction, so an alert written there rolls back with it. The studio-wide limits (TAT-55's 60 an hour, TAT-38's busy 503) refuse before any transaction; an alert for them needs a write outside the refused request and a once-per-hour key per studio. Neither is cheap, and the pilot hasn't hit them. `deploy.md`'s "Accepted denial costs" keeps saying the owner gets no signal, now naming this ruling. Rejected: a new `intake_limit` kind now.
13. **No route, isolation case or API change.** The three alerts routes and their `cases.ts` entries stay as TAT-52 made them (the `open` case's fixture stays a new-inquiry alert; row-level security covers the new columns). Rejected: a second `alert` fixture (the route is the same and the harness swaps only the slug and `:id`).
14. **Built on 65eab5f; the migration's number sorts after the newest one.** Written after TAT-18's `1791680000002`; TAT-33 then landed `1791693300000` and `1791693300001` below this branch, so the build named the migration `1791694000000_add_deposit_proof_to_staff_alerts.ts` (a later ticket takes the next free number, and says so).

## Threat notes

- **Tenant isolation.** The job runs in the payment's tenant context (`TenantJob`); every read (`Payment`, `findLive`, `ClientService.find`, `TenancyService.members`, `ArtistService.list`) and the insert go through row-level security. The composite key refuses an alert naming another studio's payment, or a payment of a different project; the unique key is per user and payment.
- **Authorization.** An alert is opened only by its own member (`AlertService.open`, unchanged: another member's or another studio's id is a 404). Its target comes from the row and a constant fragment, never from the request. The project page applies `findFor` as always: an artist reaches only a project assigned to their live profile; the email's link is a dashboard URL that needs a signed-in member.
- **A hostile guest (anyone with a deposit link).** They can send one screenshot at a time; a new one is possible only after an owner rejected the last, and the route is under `depositThrottle` (TAT-65). So a link can't be used to flood the studio's mailboxes: at most one email per member per screenshot the owner chose to reject. The email carries the client's first name as the studio stored it (from the project's client, not anything the guest typed on the deposit page), escaped in HTML.
- **A hostile member.** An artist learns of proofs only on projects assigned to them at the time; they can't open or read another member's alerts; their email links only their own project.
- **What leaves the platform.** The email: a first name, the studio's name and a dashboard link. No amount, method, reference code, token, deposit link, phone or screenshot. The subject has no client data. The job's log line is unchanged (ids only).

## Tests

- **Unit:** `alerts` (the intro's four forms, Devanagari included; the subject; the card path and its absolute URL; the two in-app lines; the target for owners and artists, and none without a project); `DepositProofMail` (addressed to the member with no `from` and no client data in the subject; the owner's version with "Check the screenshot" and the link, HTML escaped; the artist's with "Open the project"; the text version as typed); the Alerts page's empty text.
- **Functional, table:** the composite key refuses another studio's payment and a payment of another project; the subject check refuses a deposit-proof alert without a payment, with an inquiry, or with a payment but no project, and a new-inquiry alert with a payment; one alert per member and payment, a second payment fine.
- **Functional, service:** `AlertService.depositProof` (ruling 3's recipients with two owners, a removed owner, the linked artist and another artist; the owner's and the artist's email data and link; once only on a second run and again for a new payment; a rollback leaves neither alert nor email; emails off; an unassigned project and unlinked, deleted or outside profiles alert the owners only; the email names the first name only, Devanagari intact, never the last name, amount, method, phone or a deposit link).
- **Functional, job:** the job alerts the owner through the service once however often it runs; a deleted project's or a decided payment's proof alerts nobody. TAT-65's existing tests stay: the log line, and the deposit page's "a failure later in the request deletes the image and drops the alert" (the rollback with the payment).
- **Functional, HTTP:** the Alerts page shows the owner's and the artist's lines; opening redirects to `/t/<slug>/projects/<id>#payments` and marks it read; from an Inertia visit it answers 409 with that `X-Inertia-Location`.
- **Not automated:** that the browser lands on the Payments card, and how the email looks (browser pass, Mailpit).

## Browser pass (in the PR)

Dev server on `PORT=3401` and the worker with `APP_URL=http://localhost:3401 SMTP_HOST=127.0.0.1 SMTP_PORT=1025` on its command line (mail can only reach a local Mailpit). At 375 px and 1280 px, light and dark:

1. Make a deposit link on a `deposit_pending` project assigned to an artist profile linked to a second account, open it on the storefront (or `curl` the public API, as TAT-65's plan does) and send a screenshot.
2. The owner's bell shows one more; the Alerts page lists "Deposit screenshot to check", Today, unread. Tap it: the project page opens scrolled to Payments, the screenshot waiting; the bell's count drops.
3. Mailpit: one "Deposit screenshot for {studio}" email per recipient; the owner's "Check the screenshot" and the artist's "Open the project" open the project at the Payments card (after signing in, if needed).
4. Reject it, send another screenshot from the same link: a second alert and a second email.
5. As the artist: their line and email, and the project at its Payments card.
