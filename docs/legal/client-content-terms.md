# Terms for client reviews and Fresh ink

> **Draft: not legally reviewed.** Written for the product owner to take to a lawyer (Reviews and Fresh ink, ruling 12). Not published, not linked from any page, and not in force. The review page stays behind `REVIEWS_ENABLED` until a reviewed version replaces this draft.

These terms cover what a client posts through a studio's review link on TattooDrip: a rating and review, and Fresh ink (photos and videos of a tattoo). "We" is TattooDrip. "The studio" is the tattoo studio or independent artist that sent you the link. "You" is the person the link was sent to.

## 1. Who can post

1.1 Only the client of a project can post about it, through the private link the studio sent for that project. Do not share the link: whoever holds it can change or delete what you posted until it expires.

1.2 Post only about a tattoo you received at that studio, and only photos and videos you took or may share. Do not post someone else's body or face without their permission.

## 2. What you keep, and what you let us and the studio do

2.1 You keep ownership of your words, photos and videos.

2.2 You give the studio and TattooDrip a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to store, copy, resize, re-encode (for example, removing sound and location data from a video), and show your review and Fresh ink, with the name you chose, **only on the surfaces you allowed** (section 3), for as long as the content stays up.

2.3 The licence does not let the studio or us edit your words, sell your content, use it in advertising outside the studio's page and TattooDrip Discover, or give it to anyone else to use.

2.4 The studio may reply publicly to a review. The studio may choose not to show a Fresh ink post, or stop showing it, on its own page; it cannot edit it.

## 3. Consent per surface

3.1 There are two separate choices, both off until you tick them:

- **Show on the studio's page**: the studio's own TattooDrip website.
- **Show on TattooDrip Discover**: our public directory of studios and their work, offered only while the studio is listed there.

3.2 Nothing you post is shown anywhere public unless you ticked at least one box. Without either, it is kept for the studio only.

3.3 You can change either choice at any time through your link while it works, or by asking us afterwards. A withdrawn choice takes effect at once on our pages; copies kept by others (a search engine's cache, a screenshot) are outside our control, and we will ask search engines to refresh where we can.

3.4 Fresh ink shows only after the studio approves it. A review shows as soon as you submit it, where you allowed.

## 4. Your name

4.1 Public surfaces show your first name and the first letter of your last name, unless you change it (1 to 40 characters). We never show your phone number, email or full name.

4.2 Do not use someone else's name.

## 5. Deleting

5.1 You can delete your review or your post at any time through your link while it works (12 months from when the studio made it, unless the studio revokes it sooner). Deleting removes it from every public surface at once and deletes the photo and video files.

5.2 After the link stops working, ask the studio or us (contact below) and we will take it down. We keep a record that a review existed, without the photos and videos, for the studio's records and to handle disputes.

## 6. Takedowns and reports

6.1 The studio can report a review or post to us for hate, threats, personal data, being off-topic, or not coming from a client. A reported item stays up until we decide.

6.2 We can take anything down from every public surface, for a report we uphold, at your request, or where the law requires it. Your link page then says that it was taken down and why, in one fixed sentence. We may restore it if we were wrong.

6.3 Do not post: hate or harassment, threats, someone else's personal details, content you have no right to share, nudity beyond what the tattoo's placement needs, or anything unrelated to the tattoo or the studio.

## 7. Honesty

7.1 Reviews must be your own honest experience. Studios may not pay for, write, or edit reviews, and may not hide a review because it is critical; they may reply and report.

## Open questions for the legal review

- Applicable law and courts (Nepal first; any other market).
- Minimum age to post, and posts showing a minor.
- How long the record of a deleted review is kept (5.2), and privacy notice wording for the personal data in it.
- Whether the licence in 2.2 needs a separate consent for TattooDrip's own promotion (currently not granted).
- Notice-and-takedown process and response times for third-party complaints (6.2).
