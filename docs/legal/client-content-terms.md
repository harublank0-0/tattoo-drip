# DRAFT: Terms for client reviews and Fresh ink

> **Draft: not legally reviewed.** Written for the product owner to take to a lawyer (Reviews and Fresh ink, ruling 12). These terms are not linked from any page and not in force. Reviews and Fresh ink, the review page included, is live while these terms are still a draft pending legal review; a reviewed version will replace this draft.

These terms cover what a client posts through a studio's review link on TattooDrip: a rating and review, and Fresh ink (photos and videos of a tattoo). "We" is TattooDrip. "The studio" is the tattoo studio or independent artist that sent you the link. "You" is the person the link was sent to.

## 1. Who can post

1.1 Only the client of a project can post about it, through the private link the studio sent for that project. Do not share the link: whoever holds it can change or delete what you posted until it expires.

1.2 Post only about a tattoo you received at that studio, and only photos and videos you took or may share. Do not post someone else's body or face without their permission.

## 2. What you keep, and what you let us and the studio do

2.1 You keep ownership of your words, photos and videos.

2.2 You give the studio and TattooDrip a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to store, copy, resize, re-encode (for example, removing sound and location data from a video), and show your review and Fresh ink, with the name you chose, **only on the surfaces you allowed** (section 3), for as long as the content stays up.

2.3 The licence does not let the studio or us edit your words, sell your content, use it in advertising outside the studio's page and TattooDrip Discover, or give it to anyone else to use.

2.4 The studio may reply publicly to a review. The studio may choose not to show a Fresh ink post, or stop showing it, on its own page; it cannot edit it.

## 3. Consent per surface

3.1 There are two separate choices, both off until you tick them:

- **Show on the studio's page**: the studio's own TattooDrip website.
- **Show on TattooDrip Discover**: our public directory of studios and their work, offered only while the studio is listed there.

3.2 Nothing you post is shown anywhere public unless you ticked at least one box. Without either, it is kept for the studio only.

3.3 You can change either choice at any time through your link while it works, or by asking us afterwards. A withdrawn choice takes effect at once on our pages; copies kept by others (a search engine's cache, a screenshot) are outside our control, and we will ask search engines to refresh where we can.

3.4 Fresh ink shows only after the studio approves it. A review shows as soon as you submit it, where you allowed.

## 4. Your name

4.1 Public surfaces show the name you choose (1 to 40 characters). It starts as your first name and the first letter of your last name; you may change it to anything that is your own name, including your full name if you type it yourself. We never show your phone number or email, and we never show your full name unless you type it.

4.2 Do not use someone else's name.

## 5. Deleting

5.1 You can delete your review or your post at any time through your link while it works (12 months from when the studio made it, unless the studio revokes it sooner). Deleting removes it from every surface at once and deletes the photo and video files. Of a deleted review we erase the rating, the text, the name shown and your choices of where it shows; of a deleted post the photos and videos, the caption, the styles, the placement, the name shown and your choices.

5.2 What we keep after a delete is only a record that a review or post existed for that tattoo: its identifiers, its dates, the studio's decision on a post, any reply or report, any takedown record, and the dispute record of section 8 until it expires. After the link stops working, ask the studio or us (contact below) and we will delete it the same way.

## 6. Takedowns and reports

6.1 The studio can report a review to us for hate, threats, personal data, being off-topic, or not coming from a client, with a note. A reported review stays up until we decide. (A Fresh ink post needs no report: the studio can decline it or stop showing it, section 2.4.)

6.2 We can take anything down from every public surface (your review, the studio's reply to it, or your Fresh ink post), for a report we uphold, at your request, or where the law requires it. Your link page then says that your review or post was taken down and why, in one fixed sentence. We may restore it if we were wrong.

6.3 Do not post: hate or harassment, threats, someone else's personal details, content you have no right to share, nudity beyond what the tattoo's placement needs, or anything unrelated to the tattoo or the studio.

## 7. Honesty

7.1 Reviews must be your own honest experience. Studios may not pay for, write, or edit reviews, and may not hide a review because it is critical; they may reply and report.

## 8. Records for disputes

8.1 Each time you save, we keep a scrambled fingerprint (a keyed hash) of your network address and browser, with the time, on what you saved. It cannot be turned back into your address. It is never shown to anyone but TattooDrip's operators, used only to settle disputes (for example, whether two reviews came from one device), and erased after 90 days.

## 9. Private until published

9.1 Your photos and videos are stored privately: only you (through your link), the studio and TattooDrip can see them until the studio approves the post and you allow a surface. Only then are public copies made. They are deleted when the post is declined, unpublished or taken down, when you change an approved post (it waits for the studio's approval again), when you delete it, or when you withdraw your last surface; withdrawing one surface takes it off that surface at once.

## Open questions for the legal review

- Applicable law and courts (Nepal first; any other market).
- Minimum age to post, and posts showing a minor.
- How long the record of a deleted review is kept (5.2), and privacy notice wording for the personal data in it and in the dispute record (section 8).
- Whether the licence in 2.2 needs a separate consent for TattooDrip's own promotion (currently not granted).
- Notice-and-takedown process and response times for third-party complaints (6.2).
